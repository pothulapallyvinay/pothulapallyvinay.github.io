Editorial Standards

What We Look for in a Strong Guest Post

These are the qualities that make a submission stand out during editorial review.

Original Insight

The article should contain genuinely useful information, experience, analysis, examples or practical recommendations that readers cannot find in a dozen other places.

First-hand Knowledge

We prefer authors who have actually worked with the technology, process, platform, strategy or problem they are explaining, rather than summarizing someone else's article.

Clear Search Intent

The article should answer a real question or address a genuine problem that someone would actually search for or need help with.

Practical Value

Readers should leave knowing what to do differently. Teach, explain, and show, rather than just describing what a thing is at a surface level.

Strong Structure

Use meaningful H2 and H3 headings, short paragraphs, useful examples, tables when genuinely helpful, and logical progression from problem to solution.

Evidence & Human Expertise

Use authoritative sources where claims need support. The author should understand what they are discussing and be able to stand behind the article editorially.

Requirements

Guest Post Guidelines

Read these before submitting. They exist to keep the quality high for readers, which benefits everyone, including contributors.

Content must be original. Do not submit content already published elsewhere.
The article should be relevant to Companies Builder's audience of developers, designers, marketers and business teams.
Content must be useful to real readers. If a piece does not teach, explain or solve something, it is probably not a fit.
Articles should be well structured with meaningful H2/H3 headings, short paragraphs and logical flow.
Grammar, spelling and readability must be carefully checked before submission.
Claims should be accurate and supported with credible sources where appropriate.
Include original examples, insights, frameworks, data or experience wherever possible.
Images must be original, licensed, or otherwise legally usable. Do not submit copyrighted images without permission.
Do not insert misleading, manipulative or irrelevant links. Affiliate links must be disclosed.
Promotional claims should be limited. The article should educate, not advertise.
Articles may be edited for clarity, accuracy, formatting, readability and style before publication.
Submission does not guarantee publication. Companies Builder may reject or request revisions to any submission.
Write enough to cover the subject properly. Do not pad an article to hit an arbitrary word count.
AI Policy

Can I Use AI to Help Write My Article?

AI tools may be used for brainstorming, outlining, research assistance, editing or productivity, but the final article must reflect real human expertise, original reasoning and careful fact-checking.

We are not interested in mass-produced, lightly edited AI text that provides no original value. If a submission reads like a raw ChatGPT output with no genuine author insight, it will not pass editorial review.

Authors remain responsible for:

  • Accuracy and factual verification
  • Originality of ideas, analysis and recommendations
  • Citations and source attribution
  • Copyright and intellectual property
  • Technical correctness of code, data and examples
  • Final editorial quality of the published article

The focus is on usefulness and expertise. If your article genuinely helps readers and demonstrates real knowledge, how you drafted it matters less than what it contains. Companies Builder may request clarification or revision when content does not demonstrate enough subject knowledge.

Illegal, Deceptive or Unsafe Content

Anything creating legal, security, safety or trust concerns. Casino, adult and scam content is rejected automatically.

Transparency

Our Editorial Link Policy

Companies Builder does not publish articles solely for the purpose of passing ranking signals. Links within guest posts are reviewed individually by our editorial team.

Links must be relevant to the article and genuinely useful to readers. Authors should not assume that every requested external link will be published. We may:

  • Remove unnecessary or irrelevant links
  • Change anchor text for editorial clarity
  • Replace a link destination when appropriate
  • Qualify links with appropriate attributes
  • Reject links that appear promotional or manipulative
  • Request changes before publication

We do not promise guaranteed backlinks, permanent dofollow links, guaranteed SEO benefit, guaranteed rankings, or guaranteed Domain Authority growth. The editorial team makes final link decisions based on what serves readers best.

Attribution

Author Bio Guidelines

Accepted articles include an author attribution. Keep your bio professional and credible; it represents you to our readers.

Your author bio can include your full name, professional role, company or publication, relevant expertise, a short professional description, and a link to your website or professional profile where editorially appropriate.

Bios should establish credibility through honest professional context. Avoid keyword-rich exact-match anchor text, and do not turn your bio into a mini advertisement.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Guest Posting

Does Companies Builder accept guest posts?
Yes. We accept original, useful submissions related to web development, technology, web design, UI/UX, SEO, digital marketing, AI tools, content strategy and related subjects. Every submission is reviewed by our editorial team, and acceptance depends on quality, relevance and originality.
What topics can I write about?
We publish content on web development, web design and UI/UX, technology, SEO, digital marketing, AI tools and automation, WordPress and Shopify CMS platforms, and B2B growth strategies. See the Topics We Accept section for details.
Do I need professional writing experience?
No. Subject expertise and clear, useful communication matter more than being a professional journalist. If you understand the topic and can explain it clearly enough that another practitioner would find it helpful, you are qualified.
Can I submit an AI-assisted article?
AI tools may be used for brainstorming, outlining, research and editing, but the final article must reflect real human expertise and original reasoning. Mass-produced AI text without genuine author insight will not pass review. See our AI content policy for details.
Can I include links in my guest post?
Links must be relevant to the article and useful to readers. We review all external links individually and may remove, modify or qualify links that appear promotional or manipulative. We do not guarantee that every requested link will be published. Read our editorial link policy for details.
Will my article automatically be published?
No. Every submission goes through editorial review for relevance, originality, usefulness, factual quality and editorial fit. We may request revisions or decline submissions that do not meet our standards.
Can I submit an article that has already been published elsewhere?
No. Content must be original and not published elsewhere unless specifically approved under an appropriate editorial arrangement.
How long should a guest post be?
There is no arbitrary word-count target. The article should be long enough to properly cover the subject without padding. A well-structured 1,200-word article that answers a question thoroughly is better than a 3,000-word article full of filler.
Do you accept technology guest posts?
Yes, when they are original, useful and relevant to our audience. Technology topics can include web technologies, SaaS, cloud platforms, developer tools, APIs, cybersecurity, automation and business technology implementation.
Do you accept digital marketing guest posts?
Yes, when they provide genuine educational value and are not primarily promotional. Digital marketing topics can include SEO, PPC, content marketing, social media, email marketing, conversion optimization, analytics and marketing automation.
Is this a free guest post site?
Companies Builder operates an editorial contribution program. Submissions are reviewed on their merit: quality, relevance and originality. The review is not based on a paid or free model. The focus is on publishing content that genuinely helps our readers.
Is Companies Builder a guest post service?
No. Companies Builder is a web development and digital marketing agency that accepts guest contributions through an editorial program. It is not a link-selling service or bulk guest-post placement marketplace.
Do you publish health articles?
Only when the subject clearly fits our technology, web, SEO or digital-marketing focus, for example health-tech platforms, healthcare website design, healthcare SEO or digital marketing for healthcare businesses. We do not accept unrelated general health content.
Can agencies submit content?
Yes, provided the content meets our editorial requirements and is genuinely useful rather than a disguised sales page or advertisement for the agency.
Can I submit multiple articles?
Yes. If your first submission meets our editorial standards, you are welcome to pitch additional articles. We value quality over volume, so please ensure each submission is original and genuinely useful to readers.
How long does editorial review take?
Review timelines vary depending on submission volume and editorial requirements. We review submissions carefully and will contact you when we have an update on your article.
Can Companies Builder edit my article?
Yes. Accepted articles may be edited for clarity, factual accuracy, formatting, readability, structure and style consistency before publication. Significant changes are discussed with the author when possible.

Have Something Worth Teaching?

Good guest posts are not written to fill a publishing calendar. They are written because the author has something useful to share. If you have practical experience, original insights or a clear technical lesson for our readers, we'd like to see it.
